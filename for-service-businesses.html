The journey

Four steps. Miss trust, and no one calls.

This is the path a service business is actually selling along, long before anyone fills out a form.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Found in local search, on Google Maps, or through a referral someone is already trying to confirm online.

  2. 02

    Service page

    A clear page for each service, naming exactly what you do, where, and for whom, instead of one vague page trying to cover everything.

  3. 03

    Trust

    Reviews and photos of real work, the small signals that make someone believe you over the next search result.

  4. 04

    Inquiry and booking

    A call, a quote request or an online booking, connected to whatever tool you already use to manage your schedule.

Questions

What service business owners ask first.

Read every FAQ →
Do you build WordPress sites for service businesses?

No. Service-business sites are built custom-coded and static, connected to your booking tool, quote form or phone number. That keeps the site fast and simple to hand off, without a WordPress login to manage.

Can you connect my existing booking software?

Yes. The site links out to or embeds whatever booking, scheduling or quote tool you already use, so you do not have to switch tools to get a better website.

I only have one location. Is local SEO worth it?

Usually, yes. Most service businesses win or lose the first search on local signals: your Google Business Profile, reviews and a service page that clearly names what you do and where. SEO Foundations Setup covers this for up to 10 pages.

Some of your portfolio says concept build. What does that mean?

A concept build is a site design I built to show what a real rebuild could look like, for a business that has not commissioned the work. RSKIN Studio, Spiker Heating and Air, and Rainbowstar Productions are concept builds, clearly labeled wherever they are shown.
