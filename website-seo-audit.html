Who it is for

Built for the site that’s working, quietly.

This audit fits any small business site that is live but underperforming in search, not a brand-new site with nothing published yet.

Product business example

A Shopify shop with steady sales but flat search traffic

You are selling through Pinterest, social or word of mouth, but Google barely sends you anyone, and you are not sure if it is your titles, your speed, or something else entirely.

Service business example

A local business that isn’t showing up for “near me”

Your site looks fine, but you rarely hear “I found you on Google,” and you suspect your Google Business Profile or your service pages are part of the problem.

What’s included

Four checks. One clear list.

Every part of the audit exists to answer one question: what should you fix first?

  1. 01

    Technical crawl check

    Page speed, mobile usability, indexing issues, broken links, and whether your sitemap and robots.txt are set up correctly.

  2. 02

    On-page review

    Titles, headings, meta descriptions and internal links, checked against what your pages are actually trying to rank for.

  3. 03

    Local SEO check

    Your Google Business Profile, NAP (name, address, phone) consistency, and the local keywords your listing is or isn’t built around.

  4. 04

    AEO / AI-search readiness

    Schema markup, FAQ structure and entity clarity, so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can find and describe your business correctly.

What exactly do I get for $247?

A technical crawl check (speed, mobile usability, indexing, broken links, sitemap and robots.txt), an on-page review (titles, headings, meta descriptions, internal links), a local SEO check (Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, local keywords), and an AI-search readiness review (schema, FAQ structure, entity clarity). Everything is written up as one prioritized fix list, delivered within about 7 business days.

Will this guarantee my site ranks higher?

No. No honest audit can promise a ranking, since search engines and AI models weigh hundreds of factors and change constantly. What this audit guarantees is an accurate, prioritized picture of what is actually holding your site back, so you stop guessing.

Do you fix the problems for me?

This audit is the review and the plan, not the implementation. If you want the fixes done for you, the SEO Foundations Setup at $697 covers the keyword map, on-page fixes, schema, Search Console and Bing setup, sitemap, internal links and Google Business Profile optimization for up to 10 pages.

Is this only for product businesses, or service businesses too?

Both. A product business is usually reviewed for searches that end in a purchase, and a service business for searches that end in a call or a booking, including local and “near me” searches. The audit is built around which one applies to you.

What platform does my site need to be on?

Any. This audit works for a Shopify store, a custom-coded or static site, or a site built on another platform, as long as it is live and reachable on the web.
