Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page explains what information marieglowstudio.com collects, how it’s used, and who it’s shared with. It’s written in plain language rather than dense legal text, but it is a starting template, not a substitute for review by a licensed attorney familiar with your specific business and location.

Who We Are

Marie Glow Studio ("we," "us," or "our") provides web design and development, Pinterest marketing, SEO and digital strategy services, operating out of Wichita, Kansas, United States. You can reach us at hello@marieglowstudio.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you in a few specific ways:

  • Contact form (contact): When you submit our contact form, it sends the name, email address, business name, website URL, service interest, and message you entered to our own inbox. We do not sell or share this information beyond what is needed to respond to you.
  • Email signup (Flodesk): If you opt in to receive our free Pinterest checklist or other email content, your name and email address are collected and stored by our email service provider, Flodesk, so we can send you the content you requested and occasional related updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Purchases (Stripe): When you purchase a service, checkout is handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe’s own platform, not on this website. Stripe collects your name, email, billing information, and payment details directly, under its own privacy policy. We receive your order and contact details in order to deliver the service you purchased.
  • Service intake: After purchase, you’ll complete an intake form and may provide brand assets, account access, photos, or other project materials needed to complete your service. This information is used solely to deliver the service you purchased.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the service or content you requested or purchased
  • To respond to your questions or messages
  • To send you service-related updates and, if you’ve opted in, marketing emails
  • To improve our services and website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This website relies on a small number of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts: loads typefaces used across this site. Google may process the visiting device’s IP address as part of serving font files.
  • Flodesk: our email marketing platform, used for opt-in forms and email delivery.
  • Stripe: our checkout and payment processor for all paid services.

Cookies

This website does not run its own advertising trackers. Third-party services embedded on certain pages (such as Flodesk’s signup form, or an analytics snippet) may set their own cookies as part of their normal function, see each provider’s own policy for details.

Data Retention & Security

We retain information only as long as needed to deliver services, respond to inquiries, or meet legal and accounting obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email. To request that we delete correspondence or intake information we hold about you, email hello@marieglowstudio.com.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@marieglowstudio.com.
