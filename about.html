How I Work

Strategy first. Design follows.

Every project, whether it’s a website, a Pinterest account or a full strategy roadmap, goes through the same order of operations.

  1. 01

    Understand your business

    A short conversation or intake about who you serve, what you sell or offer, and how your customers actually say yes: buy, book, call or ask for a quote.

  2. 02

    Research before design

    Keyword and competitor research shapes the site structure and content from the start. Design decisions come after the strategy, not before it.

  3. 03

    Build for how people decide

    A shop gets built to end at checkout. A service business gets built to end with a call, a quote request or a booking. The platform follows the business, not the other way around.

  4. 04

    Connect the pieces

    Website, Pinterest, SEO and email are planned to hand visitors to each other, so traffic that arrives has somewhere useful to land.

  5. 05

    Stay reachable

    I answer my own inbox. Every message gets a personal reply within one to two business days, not an auto-responder.
