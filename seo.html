Three parts of SEO

Search visibility is more than Google now.

A page that isn’t built to rank, isn’t built to show up locally, and isn’t built to be read by AI is losing traffic in three different places at once.

  1. 01

    Website SEO: is your site built to be found?

    Page speed, mobile usability, indexing, broken links and a working sitemap, plus on-page basics like titles, headings, meta descriptions and internal links pointing to the right pages.

  2. 02

    Local SEO: do you show up for “near me”?

    Your Google Business Profile, Google Maps listing and NAP (name, address, phone) consistency across the web. Marie Glow Studio is based in Wichita, KS and builds local SEO for businesses there, plus service-area setups for clients across the United States.

Will SEO get my website to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise that, and any studio that does is overselling you. What SEO work can do is fix the technical and content problems that are actively keeping you out of search results and AI answers, so you are competing on a level field instead of an uneven one.

What is local SEO, and do I need it?

Local SEO is the set of signals, like your Google Business Profile, map listing and address consistency, that help you show up for searches with a location in them, including “near me.” If customers find you in person, by phone or through a service area, local SEO is worth doing alongside your general website SEO.

What is answer-engine optimization, or AEO?

AEO is making it easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to understand and quote your business correctly. That means clear schema markup, FAQ content written in plain language, and consistent facts about who you are and what you do across your site.

Do you only work with product businesses, or service businesses too?

Both. A product business is usually optimizing for searches like “handmade lavender candles” that end in a purchase. A service business is usually optimizing for searches like “HVAC repair near me” that end in a call or a booking. The SEO work is built around which one applies to you.
