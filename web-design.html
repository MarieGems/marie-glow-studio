Questions

Before you pick a starting point.

Do you only build Shopify websites?

No. Shopify is the right platform for an online store, so product businesses build there. Service businesses usually do better with a fast, custom-coded site connected to their booking tool, quote form or phone number. The business decides the platform, not the other way around.

How do I know if I need the Starter or the Complete Package?

Start with the Website Starter at $249 if you need one clear, professional page live fast, no e-commerce required. Move to the Complete Website & Brand Identity Package at $1,497 if you are launching or relaunching a full store or a multi-page service site with branding included.

Can I upgrade from the Starter later?

Yes. Upgrade to the Complete Website & Brand Identity Package within 6 months of your Starter purchase and $100 of what you paid is credited toward it.

Do you write the copy for my site?

You provide the copy for the Website Starter. The Complete Package can include copy support depending on the project. Either way, nothing goes live without your review.

What if I sell products and also take bookings?

Tell me both during intake. Most hybrid businesses still lead with one path, usually checkout or booking, and the site is built around whichever one makes you the most money.
