Use this guide whenever you make something with the Hollis & Rye name on it: a menu, a box sticker, a post, an email, a page. If something is not covered, choose the plainest option that follows the rules in sections 1, 2 and 10, and ask us.

01

Brand foundation

Hollis & Rye is a custom cake studio that tells you the price before you commit.

Promise

Custom cakes with the price in writing before you commit.

Who it is for

People ordering for a birthday, a wedding or a work event who are tired of days of emails before anyone names a price, and who need to know if their date is workable.

Personality

Plain. We say the number. Steady. We do what we wrote. Warm. Friendly without gushing. Exact. Sizes, days and dollars, not adjectives.

What we never do

Hide a price, promise a date we have not checked, use urgency tricks, or make claims we cannot back up.

Brand in one line, for the team

If a customer reads it once, they should know what it costs, when it is ready and what to do next.

02

Voice and writing

Write the way a careful person talks across a counter. Short sentences, real numbers, one action.

SayDo not say
We reply in writing within two business days.We will get back to you ASAP!
A $50 deposit holds your date and is taken off the total.Secure your dream date today.
Most custom cakes need 2 weeks. Wedding cakes need 2 months or more.Order early to avoid disappointment.
We cannot promise an allergen-free cake.Allergy friendly options available.

Rules

  • No exclamation marks and no em dashes. Use a period or a comma.
  • Numerals for money, sizes and days: $96, 8 inch, 14 days. Dates as "November 14".
  • One action per page, with the same words every time: Request a quote. The builder page may add Build your cake.
  • No made-up proof: no invented reviews, awards or results. If a number is an example, say so next to it.
  • Explain a form: "A form is a request, not an order. Nothing is charged until you approve the quote."

Words we use and avoid

Use: quote, written, deposit, balance, pickup, delivery, lead time, size, serves. Avoid: dream, bespoke, curated, indulgent, artisanal, luxury, magical, perfect, ASAP.

Sample quote message

Cake$96

06

Layout and spacing

  • Page width: content up to 1040 px. Sections use a narrow label column on the left (200 px) and text on the right, with a 32 px gap. On phones this becomes one column.
  • Section spacing: 40 to 80 px top and bottom, separated by a 1 px Line divider. No cards inside cards and no grids of identical cards. Lists of things use numbered rows.
  • Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80 px.
  • Corners: buttons are pills, option chips 12 px, form fields 6 px, panels square.
  • Shadows: only the hard 4 px Cocoa shadow on buttons. No blurs or glows.
  • Breakpoint: 860 px. Below it, go single column and keep the sticky header to the logo and the quote button.

08

Imagery and illustration

  • Photography: soft window light from the left, shallow depth of field, shot slightly above eye level, plain sage or warm wood surface, no people, no text. Props limited to a stand, a linen napkin, fruit or flowers that match the cake.
  • Real photos first. A real bakery should always use photos of its own cakes. Generated pictures are for concept demos only and must never be passed off as baked work.
  • Illustration: flat shapes, 3 px Cocoa outline, Frosting and Terracotta fills, a Line-colored ground shadow. One cake per picture.
  • 3D cake views: plain geometry, soft light, cream frosting, no glitter or glow, a wood board underneath.
  • Alt text: describe the cake in one sentence. Do not start with "image of".
  • Files: WebP with a JPG fallback, under 200 KB where possible, with width and height set.

Photography examples

These ten concept images set the standard for the whole system: same sage backdrop, same soft light, a cake as the only subject. They were generated with AI for this fictional demo. A real bakery uses its own photos and holds them to the same direction.

Finish details

Crop rules: collection images 3:2, finish details 1:1, the hero 4:5, the share image 1200 x 630 with the right side left empty for text. Keep the subject off the outer 10% so crops stay safe.

09

Motion

  • Timing: 100 ms for taps, 250 ms for changes, 450 ms for entrances. Easing cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1).
  • Allowed: a rise and fade on the first headline, quote lines appearing one by one like a receipt, the builder easing between choices, a count-up on prices, and a slow turn on the 3D cake.
  • Not allowed: parallax, scroll hijacking, autoplay video with sound, bouncing, anything that moves while someone is reading.
  • Reduced motion: turn all of it off. Content must be fully visible with no animation and with JavaScript off.
10

Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA. These are the working rules.

  • Text contrast follows section 4. Do not add new color pairs without measuring them.
  • Touch targets at least 48 px. Visible 3 px focus ring on everything you can tab to.
  • Every form field has a visible label. Errors say what to fix in words.
  • No information by color alone (calendar days use a word and a pattern).
  • Everything works with a keyboard. The 3D cake turns with the left and right arrow keys.
  • Respect reduced motion. Alt text on every real image.
  • Payment and card details are never collected on a brochure page. Use the payment provider's own page.

What was tested on the demo site

Contrast of every text style, keyboard use of the builder and calendar, no horizontal scroll at phone width, reduced motion and JavaScript off. Not tested: screen readers, Safari and Firefox, real phones.
