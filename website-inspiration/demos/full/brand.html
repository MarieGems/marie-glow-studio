Logo

Wordmark and mark.

Hollis & Rye

Primary wordmark in Young Serif. The ampersand is the only colored letter, in rust #9A4222.

Monogram for small spaces, such as a profile photo or a cake box seal.

Keep clear space equal to the height of the H on every side. Do not stretch, outline or add shadows.

Type

Typography.

Young Serif for headings

Karla for body text at 18px and up, with 1.6 line height. Labels are Karla 600, uppercase, letter-spaced. Both load from Google Fonts. Fallbacks are Georgia and the system sans.

Button

The one button.

Request a quote

Pill shape, 3px cocoa border, 4px hard shadow, presses down on hover, at least 48px tall. One action, always the same words.

Voice

How the words sound.

Plain and specific. Prices, days and sizes instead of adjectives. No exclamation marks, no hype, no promises we cannot keep.

Voice

Say this, not that.

Write the way a careful person talks across a counter. Short sentences, real numbers, one action.

SayWe reply in writing within two business days.

Do not sayWe will get back to you ASAP!

SayA $50 deposit holds your date and is taken off the total.

Do not saySecure your dream date today.

SayMost custom cakes need 2 weeks. Wedding cakes need 2 months or more.

Do not sayOrder early to avoid disappointment.

SayWe cannot promise an allergen-free cake.

Do not sayAllergy friendly options available.

Use: quote, written, deposit, balance, pickup, delivery, lead time, size, serves.
Avoid: dream, bespoke, curated, indulgent, artisanal, luxury, magical, perfect, ASAP.

No exclamation marks and no em dashes. Numerals for money, sizes and days. One action per page, always Request a quote. No made-up reviews, awards or results.
