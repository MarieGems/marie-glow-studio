FAQ

Before you start your project.

Is this a template website?

No, every site is designed and built from scratch around your branding, content, colors and images.

I already have a logo and branding, do I still get the branding side?

The package covers both branding and website design, but if you have existing branding you want to keep, let us know during intake and the focus shifts to the website side instead.

Do you build on Shopify or custom code?

Whichever fits your business. Online stores get a full Shopify build with e-commerce. Service businesses get a fast custom-coded site wired to your booking tool, quote form or phone number.

How many products can this handle?

For a Shopify build, up to 20 items are included in the setup: titles, descriptions, images, collections, and basic SEO for each.

How long does it take?

Roughly 2 to 3 weeks, depending on how quickly you can provide photos and content. That covers branding, the full site build, testing and your feedback. If you are wondering why a website is not a one-day project, here is why.

Is this refundable?

No, it is a personalized digital service, non-refundable once work begins.

Can this credit toward a Digital Strategy Roadmap I already bought?

Yes. If you book the Complete Website & Brand Identity Package within 60 days of your Digital Strategy Roadmap, the $397 you paid for the Roadmap is credited toward this package.
