What exactly do I get for $247?

A technical crawl check (speed, mobile usability, indexing, broken links, sitemap and robots.txt), an on-page review (titles, headings, meta descriptions, internal links), a local SEO check (Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, local keywords), and an AI-search readiness review (schema, FAQ structure, entity clarity). Will this guarantee my site ranks higher?

No. No honest audit can promise a ranking, since search engines and AI models weigh hundreds of factors and change constantly. What this audit guarantees is an accurate, prioritized picture of what is actually holding your site back, so you stop guessing.

Do you fix the problems for me?

This audit is the review and the plan, not the implementation. If you want the fixes done for you, the SEO Foundations Setup at $697 covers the keyword map, titles and meta descriptions, schema markup, Search Console and Bing setup, sitemap, internal links and Google Business Profile optimization for up to 10 pages.

Is this only for product businesses, or service businesses too?

Both. A product business is usually reviewed for searches that end in a purchase, and a service business for searches that end in a call or a booking, including local and “near me” searches. The audit is built around which one applies to you.
