Is this a contract?

No, it’s month-to-month with no minimum commitment, though three to six months is recommended to see real traction. Payment is billed in advance and non-refundable once work begins for that month.

Can I purchase without a consultation?

Yes. No consultation or application required. Choose your tier, complete checkout, and follow the post-purchase instructions.

Do I need an existing Pinterest business account?

Yes, it’s what gives you access to analytics and Pinterest’s business features. If you don’t have one yet, start with Pinterest Account Setup first.

Will you need access to my accounts?

Yes, to Pinterest, your website, scheduling tools, Shopify or Canva depending on your account. Secure collaborator or delegated-access methods are used whenever the platform supports them.

Can you guarantee more traffic or sales?

No ethical Pinterest specialist can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, followers, clicks, traffic or sales. The focus is building strong SEO foundations, strategic content, and consistent long-term visibility. Results may take several weeks or months of consistent effort.
