Pinterest is a search engine for what people are planning to do next.

Not a social feed

People use Pinterest to decide, not to scroll.

  1. 01

    It works for products

    Shops, makers and product brands use Pinterest for exactly the searches you would expect: gift ideas, seasonal products, “outfits for,” “gifts for.” A pin points straight to the product or collection that matches.

  2. 02

    It works for services too

    Someone planning a wedding searches Pinterest for venues and florists. Someone renovating a bathroom searches for tile ideas and contractors. Salons, planners, coaches and home-service businesses all show up in the same kind of inspiration search, months before that person is ready to book.

  3. 03

    It keeps paying off

    A social post is seen for a day or two. A well-built pin, with the right title, description and keywords, keeps getting found in search for months after you publish it.

  4. 04

    It feeds your website

    Every pin should land on the exact page that matches what someone searched: a product, a service page, a blog post or a booking link. Pinterest is the discovery layer that sends people to the site that closes the sale.

Does Pinterest marketing only work for online shops?

No. Pinterest works for any business someone plans ahead for, which is most service businesses too. People search Pinterest for wedding venues, salons, home renovation ideas, meal planners and coaches the same way they search for products, so a salon or a planner can rank for those searches the same way a shop ranks for a product.

Do I need a Pinterest business account?

Yes, it is what gives you access to analytics and Pinterest’s business features. Pinterest Account Setup is available if you do not have one yet.

Can I purchase without a consultation?

Yes. No consultation or application is required. Choose your service, complete checkout, and follow the post-purchase instructions.

What if I purchase the wrong service?

If your intake form shows another service would fit better, you will be contacted before any work begins.

Will you need access to my accounts?

Account Setup and Monthly Management may require access to Pinterest, your website, scheduling tools, Shopify or Canva. Secure collaborator or delegated-access methods are used whenever the platform supports them.

Can you guarantee more traffic or sales?

No ethical Pinterest specialist can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, followers, clicks, traffic or sales. The focus is building strong SEO foundations, strategic content, and consistent long-term visibility. Pinterest results can take several weeks or months of consistent effort.
