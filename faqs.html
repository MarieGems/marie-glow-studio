General

The basics.

What is Marie Glow Studio?

Marie Glow Studio is a digital studio in Wichita, KS that helps product and service small businesses with web design and development, Pinterest marketing, SEO and digital strategy. The work is done by one person, start to finish, so your website, search visibility and marketing are planned together instead of in separate pieces.

Do you work with service businesses, or only product businesses?

Both. A product business needs a clear path from discovery to checkout. A service business needs a clear path from discovery to an inquiry, a call or a booking. Every site and strategy is built around the way your own customers actually say yes.

Do I need a website?

Yes, if you want Pinterest, search or referrals to turn into anything. Whatever brings someone to you should land them on a page built to answer their question and give them a next step: a product, a service page, a booking link or a contact form.

Do you only work with businesses in Wichita?

No. Marie Glow Studio is based in Wichita, Kansas and works remotely with small businesses across the United States. Local Wichita businesses get the same service, plus local SEO that is built with the area in mind.

How will we communicate?

You work directly with one person: me. Questions, missing information, access needs and updates go through the contact method set up during onboarding, and I reply personally within one to two business days.

Do you only build Shopify websites?

No. Shopify is one option, and it is usually the right one for an online store. Service businesses often do better with a fast custom-coded site connected to their booking tool or quote form. The business problem decides the platform, not the other way around.

What is included in the Website Starter?

The Website Starter, $249, is one professional page shaped around your business: a shop or a service, with the buy button, booking link or quote form that fits how your customers say yes. It is live in about five business days.

What is included in the Complete Website & Brand Identity package?

The Complete Website & Brand Identity package, $1,497, is full branding plus a multi-page site, built on Shopify for a store or custom-coded for a service business. It includes the visual identity work a single-page Starter site doesn’t need.

Do you redesign existing websites, or only build new ones?

Both. A redesign follows the same process as a new build: research and strategy first, then design. Existing content, branding and SEO work that is still working gets carried forward instead of thrown out.

Can’t I just build my website with AI?

You can, and I use AI myself. The real question is whether you want to make every decision it needs to get right: who the site is for, what visitors should see first, how it shows up in search and how it works on a phone. I use AI as a tool; my work is the strategy, direction, editing and quality control around it. If you would rather do it yourself, go for it. If you get stuck wondering whether you are building the right thing, that is where the Website Starter and Complete Website Package come in. The longer answer is here: can AI build a website?

Is Pinterest a social media platform or a search engine?

Pinterest is a visual search and discovery platform. People use it to search for products, services, ideas and solutions. Unlike traditional social media, an optimized pin can keep appearing in search results and driving traffic long after it was published.

Which Pinterest service should I choose?

Choose the Pinterest SEO Audit, $197, if you have an account and want to know what is working and what needs improvement. Choose Pinterest Account Setup, $497, for a new account or to rebuild one on a stronger foundation. Choose Pinterest Management, $497 to $1,097 a month, for ongoing strategy and content handled for you.

What is included in the Pinterest SEO Audit?

Your $197 audit reviews profile name and bio, boards, keywords, existing pins, account organization, website connection and analytics, and ends with personalized recommendations and a prioritized 30-day action plan.

What is included in Pinterest Account Setup?

The $497 setup builds or rebuilds your account foundation: keyword research and competitor analysis, an SEO-focused profile, content pillars, board strategy across up to 10 boards, 10 custom pins with SEO titles, descriptions, alt text and UTM tracking, and a 30-day launch plan.

What can Monthly Pinterest Management include?

Depending on the tier ($497, $797 or $1,097 a month), every tier includes keyword research, content planning, custom branded pin graphics with SEO titles and descriptions, alt text, scheduling, board maintenance, a monthly analytics review, one revision round and email support. Higher tiers add pins per month and extras such as a monthly strategy call on Pro.

How quickly does Pinterest marketing work?

Pinterest is a long-term platform. Some pins gain visibility quickly, others take weeks or months. Results vary based on your account, niche, keywords, content, website and publishing consistency.

What is included in the Website SEO Audit?

The $247 Website SEO Audit covers a technical crawl (speed, mobile, indexing, broken links, sitemap and robots.txt), an on-page review (titles, headings, meta, internal links), a local SEO check (Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, local keywords) and an AEO/AI-search readiness check (schema, FAQ structure, entity clarity), delivered as a written, prioritized fix list within about 7 business days.

What is included in SEO Foundations Setup?

The $697 SEO Foundations Setup covers up to 10 pages: a keyword map, titles and meta descriptions, schema markup, Search Console and Bing setup, a sitemap, internal linking and Google Business Profile optimization.

What is AEO, and do you do it?

Yes, AEO is part of the SEO work here, where it fits. AEO, answer engine optimization, means structuring a page so AI assistants and answer features like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers can read it and pull out a clear answer: direct answers near the top of a page, FAQ sections that match what people actually ask, consistent facts about your business and schema markup. The Website SEO Audit includes an AI-search readiness review (schema, FAQ structure and entity clarity), and SEO Foundations Setup adds schema markup and the technical groundwork. No one can promise an AI tool will mention or cite you, so the work stays with what you control: structured content, consistent facts across the web, local signals like your Google Business Profile, and being able to see how you show up. For the plain-English version, read SEO vs AEO vs GEO.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical SEO service can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, clicks or sales. Results depend on your niche, competition, content and website, and on how consistently the recommended changes get implemented. The focus is building strong, honest foundations.
