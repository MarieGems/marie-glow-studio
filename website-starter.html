FAQ

Before you reach out.

Is this a template website?

No, it is designed and built from scratch around your branding, content, colors and images.

Can I add more pages?

The Starter itself is one page, but you can add extra pages individually for $79 each. Need a full multi-page site from the start? Move into the Complete Website & Brand Identity Package instead.

How long will it take?

Delivered within 5 business days of your completed intake, guaranteed, or your revision round is free.

What if I need a small edit after it’s live?

Your first 30 days of small text or photo edits are free. After that, it is $25 per edit or $15 a month for up to two, and you never have to touch the code yourself.

Can you write my website copy?

Not included in the Starter. You provide the copy. Ask about copywriting separately if you would like help with it.

Can I sell products through this website?

Not with the Starter. It is static HTML, not a store. If you need e-commerce, the Complete Website & Brand Identity Package sets you up on Shopify instead.

Can I take bookings or quote requests through this website?

Yes. A service business’s Starter can connect to a booking tool, quote form or your phone number directly, the same way a bigger custom-coded build would.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, brand colors and fonts if you have them, business info, photos, copy, contact details, social links and your call to action.

How many revisions are included?

One round, made after you review your preview.

Where does the site live?

You get static files you can host on your own domain, GitHub Pages, Netlify, or wherever you prefer, no ongoing platform fee required.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade to the $1,497 Complete Website & Brand Identity Package within 6 months of your Starter purchase and $100 of what you paid is credited toward it.

I only need help with Pinterest, not a website, is that here too?

Start with the $197 Pinterest SEO Audit instead, a full account audit and a ranked 30-day plan.
