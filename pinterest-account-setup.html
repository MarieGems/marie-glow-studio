Do I need to already have a Pinterest account?

No, this service creates one from scratch if you don’t have one yet, or rebuilds an existing account that was never set up strategically.

Will you need access to my accounts?

Yes, likely to Pinterest, your website, Shopify or Canva depending on what your setup needs. Secure collaborator or delegated-access methods are used whenever the platform supports them.

What if this turns out to be the wrong service for me?

If your intake form shows another service would fit better, you’ll be contacted before any work begins.

Do you keep managing my account after setup?

No, this is the foundation, not ongoing posting. If you want it kept moving after setup, see Pinterest Management.

Can you guarantee more traffic or sales?

No ethical Pinterest specialist can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, followers, clicks, traffic or sales. The focus is building a strong SEO foundation and strategic content for consistent long-term visibility. Results may take several weeks or months of consistent effort.
