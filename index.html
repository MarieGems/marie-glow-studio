What your website is for

Looking good is the starting line. Getting chosen is the job.

  1. 01

    Easier to find

    You are easier to find on Google and Pinterest, and easier for AI tools to understand, when someone searches for what you sell or the service you offer, including the searches that end in “near me.”

  2. 02

    Easier to understand

    Within a few seconds on your site, a visitor knows what you do, who it is for and where you work. No guessing, no digging through menus.

  3. 03

    Easier to choose

    Reviews, photos of real work, clear prices and a site that holds up on a phone. The small signals that make someone trust you over the next result.

  4. 04

    Easier to say yes

    Buy, book, call, ask for a quote or join your list. Whatever a yes looks like for your business, the next step is right there.

What is Marie Glow Studio?

Marie Glow Studio is a digital studio in Wichita, KS that helps product and service small businesses with web design and development, Pinterest marketing, SEO and digital strategy. The work is done by one person, start to finish, so your website, search visibility and marketing are planned together instead of in separate pieces.

Do you only build Shopify websites?

No. Shopify is one option, and it is usually the right one for an online store. Service businesses often do better with a fast custom-coded site connected to their booking tool or quote form. The business problem decides the platform, not the other way around.

Do you work with service businesses, or only product businesses?

Both. A product business needs a clear path from discovery to checkout. A service business needs a clear path from discovery to an inquiry, a call or a booking. Every site and strategy is built around the way your own customers actually say yes.

Do I need all four services?

No. Most businesses start with the one piece that is holding them back the most, often a website or an audit. The four services are designed to connect, so anything added later fits what is already built instead of starting over.

How much does it cost to work with Marie Glow Studio?

Audits start at $197 for Pinterest and $247 for website SEO. The Website Starter is $249, SEO Foundations Setup is $697, and the Complete Website and Brand Identity package is $1,497. Monthly Pinterest management runs $497 to $1,097. Every price is listed on its service page.

Do you only work with businesses in Wichita?

No. Marie Glow Studio is based in Wichita, Kansas and works remotely with small businesses across the United States. Local Wichita businesses get the same service, plus local SEO that is built with the area in mind.
