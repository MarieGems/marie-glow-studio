The journey

Five steps. Miss one, and the sale slips.

This is the path every product business is actually selling along, whether the site is built for it or not.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Found on Pinterest or in search while someone is actively planning what to buy. Keyword-led pins and clear product titles get you into that moment.

  2. 02

    Product page

    Lands on the exact product or collection that matches what they searched, with real photos and details that answer the obvious questions.

  3. 03

    Cart

    Adding to cart and seeing what is in it works cleanly on a phone, where most of this traffic actually arrives.

  4. 04

    Purchase

    Checkout has no surprise steps, unclear shipping costs, or dead links that make someone abandon at the last second.

  5. 05

    Retention

    Email brings them back for the next order instead of leaving that relationship to chance.

Do you build on Shopify or something else?

For a product business selling online, Shopify is usually the right platform, and it is what every product-business site in the portfolio is built on. It handles cart, checkout, inventory and payments without needing separate plugins bolted together.

I already have a Shopify store. Can you improve it instead of rebuilding it?

Yes. A Website SEO Audit or a Digital Strategy Roadmap can find the specific pages or steps that are costing you sales without a full rebuild. Many product businesses only need one or two things fixed, not a new site.

What is the smallest way to start?

The $249 Website Starter is the smallest full build. If you already have a site, the $197 Pinterest SEO Audit or $247 Website SEO Audit are smaller starting points that come with a written, ranked fix list.

Do you help with the Pinterest side too, or only the website?

Both, and they are meant to work together. Pinterest sends the discovery traffic; the website has to be ready to turn it into a purchase. Pinterest Account Setup is $497 and monthly Management runs from $497.
