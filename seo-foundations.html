What exactly is included for $697?

A keyword map for your site, titles and meta descriptions rewritten for up to 10 pages, schema markup, Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools setup, a sitemap, internal linking between your pages, and Google Business Profile optimization if you have a local or service-area business.

Do I need the Website SEO Audit first?

It helps but is not required. The audit tells you what is wrong before anything gets built; SEO Foundations Setup does the building. Many people go straight to the setup when they already know their site needs the work done, not just diagnosed.

What if my site has more than 10 pages?

Ten pages covers most small business sites: home, a handful of product, collection or service pages, about and contact. If your site has more pages, you choose the 10 that matter most and those are the pages covered.

Will this guarantee higher rankings?

No. No one can guarantee a ranking, and this setup will not promise one. It gives your site the technical and on-page foundation search engines and AI assistants need to understand and surface you correctly, which is the groundwork every ranking depends on.

What platform does this work on?

Shopify stores and custom-coded or static sites both work. The specific steps differ by platform, but the keyword map, schema, Search Console setup and Google Business Profile work apply either way.
